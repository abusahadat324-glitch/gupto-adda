Getting ready…

  • Locked on your device.Every message is encrypted with AES-256-GCM in your browser before it is saved.
  • One passphrase, one room.For a private chat, share a passphrase with one friend. For a group, share it with everyone.
  • The passphrase stays with you.It is never sent anywhere or stored. If everyone forgets it, the messages cannot be recovered.
  • What stays visible.The time of each message and how many there are. Names and text do not.
